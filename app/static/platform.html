Why Choose a Platform Approach?

Traditional development requires juggling multiple tools - an IDE, a separate CI/CD system, communication tools, deployment platforms, and more. Each tool switch costs time and mental energy.

DevHub eliminates this friction by bringing everything into one cohesive platform. Your code, builds, tests, reviews, and deployments all happen in the same place, with the same UI, and the same authentication.
